How I Work

A few rules I build by

01

A pipeline that fails loudly is fine. One that succeeds wrongly is the enemy.

Validation and alerting live inside the pipeline, not around it, so a bad run pages someone instead of quietly shipping a wrong number.

02

Design for the failure mode, not the happy path.

Backup tables, validate-then-swap cutovers, and idempotent reruns mean downstream teams never see a half-loaded table, even when something upstream breaks.

03

The first deliverable should be a number leadership cannot ignore.

Whether it is untracked spend or a reconciliation gap, I start with the metric that proves the work matters, then build the platform underneath it.

04

Precision people can trust beats confidence they cannot.

I would rather say "roughly" out loud and defend an honest estimate than round a number I cannot back up ten minutes into a follow-up question.
